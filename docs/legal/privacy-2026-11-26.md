We are the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This policy tells you what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

The short version:

- Your health information is hidden unless you choose to share it, and you decide who sees it.
- We never sell your information and never use your health information for advertising.
- You can see, correct, download and delete your information.

## 3. Your health information

Health information is special personal information under POPIA (section 26). We process it only with your explicit, separate consent (section 27(1)(a)), which we ask for right before your first health question, never during sign-up. You never have to add any.

**You decide who sees it.** Health details are hidden by default. You can change this at any time:

| Setting | Who can see your health details |
| --- | --- |
| Hidden (default) | Nobody |
| After a match | Only people you have matched with |
| Shared in chat | Only the person you send a disclosure card to |
| On profile | Anyone who views your profile |

Taking access away works straight away. If the other person has your details open, they disappear from their screen at once, or within about a minute if their device is offline.

**How we protect it:**

- It is stored in a separate, locked-down database, encrypted with a key that belongs to you alone.
- Only one part of our system can read it, and it checks your setting every time.
- Every time anyone looks at it, allowed or refused, we record who, why and when. You can see who has viewed your health details.
- It never appears in notifications, emails, SMS, logs, error reports, analytics or support tools, and is never used to rank or advertise.
- Only members who share their own condition can use condition-based filters, and we hide results that are small enough to reveal someone.
- Our staff see it only when a safety report requires it, and that access is recorded and time-limited.

**What we cannot prevent.** Someone you share with could photograph their screen. Our phone apps block or blur screenshots where the device allows it, but a web browser cannot. Our rules forbid sharing other members' health information, and you can report it as a high-priority safety issue.

**Withdrawing consent.** You can withdraw your health consent at any time in settings. We then delete your health information by destroying its key, and remove it from backups within 30 days. Your account stays open.

## 4. The selfie check (biometric information)

The selfie check shows other members you are a real person. It is free, and you need it before you can send messages; everything else works without it. Selfie images are biometric information, which POPIA treats as special personal information, so we ask for your separate consent right before the camera opens.

- **What we take:** a selfie and a few frames from a short video. We do not ask for an ID document.
- **Who checks it:** our verification provider, Smile ID, acting on our instructions. It receives only the images and a random code made for that attempt, never your name, number, date of birth or anything about your health.
- **What we keep:** whether the check passed, a short reason code and the provider's reference. We keep no face templates or scores.
- **How long:** we keep the images encrypted for up to 30 days, so a person can review them if the automatic check does not work for you. Then we delete them and ask Smile ID to delete its copy.
- **If the check does not work for you:** after 3 failed attempts you can ask for a person to review it. Some people, for example with facial differences or treatment-related changes, are not served well by automatic checks.
- **Withdrawing consent:** we delete the images at once, ask Smile ID to delete its copy and remove your verified badge.

## 5. Who receives your information

**We never sell your information, rent it out or share it for advertising.**

**Other members** see what you put on your profile: display name, age (not your date of birth), approved photos, bio and the other fields you fill in, a verified badge if you have one, and a distance rounded to at least 1 km. They never see your phone number, exact location or date of birth. Health details follow your settings (section 3). In private mode, only people you have liked can see your profile.

**Messages** are end-to-end encrypted: only you and the person you write to can read them. Our servers hold them encrypted until delivered, for at most 30 days. If you report a conversation, your app sends us only the messages you choose, so our safety team can review them.

**Service providers (operators)** process information for us under written agreements that require security and confidentiality (POPIA sections 20 and 21). Each receives only what its task needs:

| Provider | Task | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting provider (named here before we open to members) | Servers, databases and file storage | Stored, encrypted data |
| SMS provider (named here before we open to members) | Sends sign-in codes | Your mobile number and a code. The text never names the app or any condition |
| Email provider (named here before we open to members) | Sends emails you ask for | Your email address. Emails never contain health information |
| Smile ID | Selfie check | Selfie images and a random code (section 4) |

We will list any new provider here before it receives personal information.

**Authorities.** We disclose information to police, courts or regulators only when the law requires it and the request is valid, for example a court order or a direction under the Cybercrimes Act, 2020. We give the minimum required. Where the law allows, we tell you first.

**If the business changes hands.** A buyer would have to keep this policy's promises, and we would tell you before your information moved.

## 6. Information sent outside South Africa

We aim to keep your information in South Africa, and will name the country where our servers are before we open to members. Your health information is stored only in our separate health database in South Africa and never goes to any provider abroad.

Where a provider processes information outside South Africa, we send it only if POPIA section 72 allows: the recipient is bound by a law, binding corporate rules or a written agreement giving protection substantially similar to POPIA, or you have consented to the transfer.

Selfie images may be processed by Smile ID outside South Africa, including in the United States. Before that happens we will have signed terms with Smile ID that give POPIA-level protection, and we will tell you where the images go in the consent screen before the camera opens.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, unless a law requires longer (POPIA section 14). Then we delete or destroy it so it cannot be rebuilt.

| Information | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in codes | 5 minutes |
| Undelivered encrypted messages | Until delivered, at most 30 days |
| Selfie images | Up to 30 days after the check |
| Photo uploads that were never finished | Deleted within a day |
| Backups of the health database | At most 30 days |
| Your account, profile, photos, health information and consent records | Until you delete your account |
| Records of safety reports and bans | As long as needed to keep a banned person off Chronos Place and to deal with any legal claim, then deleted |

**When you delete your account,** we stop showing your profile at once, end every session and delete your information from our systems within 30 days. Your health information becomes unreadable straight away, because we destroy the key that unlocks it, including for copies in backups.

**What we keep after deletion:** a one-way code made from your mobile number (it cannot be turned back into the number), so that an account closed for being under 18, or banned for serious harm, cannot simply sign up again; and any record a law requires us to keep, such as payment records for tax purposes, for the period that law sets.

## 8. How we keep it safe

We take appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information (POPIA section 19), including:

- encryption in transit and at rest, with separate keys for each member's sensitive information (mobile number, selfie images, health information);
- end-to-end encryption for messages;
- photos re-made on our servers with all hidden data removed, such as the location your phone may store in a photo;
- sign-in codes and notifications that never name the app or a condition on a lock screen;
- staff access only where their role needs it, recorded and reviewed;
- logs that hold no health information, and regular security testing.

No system is perfectly secure. **If we have reason to believe someone has accessed your information without authority, we will tell the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible** (POPIA section 22), in writing, with what happened, what we are doing and what you can do to protect yourself.

You help too: keep your phone locked, do not share sign-in codes, and sign out of shared devices.

## 9. Automated decisions, marketing, cookies and children

**Automated decisions (POPIA section 71).** We use automated tools to suggest profiles, to check photos and messages for content that breaks our rules, and to run the selfie check. We do not take a decision that has legal consequences for you, or affects you to a substantial degree, based solely on automated processing. A person reviews any suspension or ban before it takes effect, except where an immediate step is needed to protect someone, which a person then reviews. You can ask for a human review of a failed selfie check and appeal any moderation decision, and tell us why you think it is wrong.

**Marketing.** We send product updates or community news by email, SMS or other electronic message only if you have said yes (POPIA section 69). Not ticking a box never counts as yes. Every message tells you how to stop, and you can say no at any time in settings. Messages about your account, safety or these policies are not marketing. We never use health information for marketing.

**Cookies and similar technology.** The website uses only what it needs to work: sign-in cookies that keep you signed in and tie your session to your device, and your light or dark mode choice, stored in your browser. We use no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics cookies.

**Children.** Chronos Place is only for people aged 18 or older. If you tell us a date of birth under 18, we close the account, do not keep the date and make your number unreadable. If you think a child is using the service, tell us at **legal@ahavaon88.co.za**.

## 10. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

- **know** what personal information we hold about you and who has received it (section 23);
- **correct** it, or have it **deleted** if it is inaccurate, out of date, excessive or no longer needed (section 24);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests (section 11(3)), and to direct marketing (section 11(3)(b));
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that already happened (section 11(2)(b));
- **not be subject** to solely automated decisions with substantial effects (section 71);
- **complain** to the Information Regulator.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, change who sees your health details, see who viewed them, withdraw consents and delete your account. For anything else, contact our Information Officer at **legal@ahavaon88.co.za**. We may ask you to prove who you are first, and will answer within a reasonable time. Access requests follow our PAIA manual, available free from our Information Officer on request; confirming whether we hold information about you is free, and a copy of it may carry only the fee that manual sets.

**Complaints.** Please tell us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the Information Regulator at any time:

- General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za, 010 023 5200 or 0800 017 160 (toll free)
- Address: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191
